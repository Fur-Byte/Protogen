## Finding: energy checkpoint range (Medium, reproduced)

In `firmware/esp32/src/WearerIO.cpp`, the worker loads a uint32_t checkpoint
into a double accumulator and adds valid INA228 energy deltas. The subsequent
unconditional uint32_t cast can be out of range. A real worker execution
with modeled NVS returning UINT32_MAX and valid INA samples reproduced
GCC float-cast-overflow at the checkpoint conversion (exit 1).
[PR #69](https://github.com/Fur-Byte/Protogen/pull/69) saturates used-energy
accumulation, leaving independent measured energy and FULL qualification
semantics intact. All five worker modes passed with deadlines disabled and
enabled under ASan/UBSan/float-cast-overflow (10 executions).
