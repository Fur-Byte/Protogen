## Boundaries for later work

- **Inherited and third-party.** Exclude Feronium's original sketch spans, bitmaps, derived art and schematic, the archive, ZillionRoss head assets, the BSD MAX7219 fork, vendored Mbed TLS and mlkem-native, and pinned dependencies. Check ancestry before editing mixed-origin firmware. Feronium redistribution permission and the model's exact license version are unresolved. Linked FFT and Entropy carry GPL obligations. The repository has no blanket license grant. [Notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#L3-L33)
- **Generated and experimental.** Treat build outputs, generated configuration metadata and v4 vectors as products of their generators. Keep frozen frame/replay fixtures and qualification artifacts as evidence. S31/v4 and PQ research stay isolated from C3/v3. Keep Animation V2, timelines, Audio V2 and efficiency candidates opt-in. Retain PR #13's off-grid audio checks, independent I²C retry tests, and distinct raw-capture hashes. [Target status](../README.md#L7-L15) · [Audio regression](P7_AUDIO_BEHAVIOR.md#carrier-sensitivity-regression) · [Capture checks](../scripts/analyze-efficiency.py#L215-L224)
- **Physical qualification is BLOCKED.** F3 requires an identified assembly, named installed board roles, matching image hashes and feature flags against an independent candidate. Preserve legacy evidence reading and the reviewed artifacts. Timing/ADC, optical mapping and brightness, mounted-microphone audio, power/thermal, RF, recovery and a connected one-hour trial need measured evidence on the fitted hardware. Its availability is unconfirmed. [Candidate matching](../scripts/qualification.py#L238-L260) · [Bench sequence](P8_HARDWARE_QUALIFICATION.md#prioritized-bench-preparation)

For implementation branches, use focused local checks and one automatic fast PR run per stable candidate. Reuse exact-SHA evidence and follow the repository's manual full-run rule after fast CI passes. [CI policy](../AGENTS.md)
